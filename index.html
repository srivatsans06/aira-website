What AIRA Stands For

AIRA merges traditional education with AI, promoting resilience and ongoing enhancement in learning. Tap a letter to explore.

    The Problem

    Gap between Traditional Educational Practices and the Evolving AI-driven World, and a lack of Kaizen & Resilience. Focus: Global Indian International School, Whitefield, students and teachers, from the 2024 academic session onwards.

    AI Literacy Crisis

    75%

    Kaizen Gap

    of students focus on grades over learning, missing continuous improvement opportunities.

    TRIVENI Clubs

    Fusing art, wellness and expression for resilient, holistic learning. 107 club participants in total.

    MOUN Club · Break the Silence

    Spreads mental health awareness through education and support, encourages self-care and open, inclusive dialogue, and partners with NGOs such as Thayi Mane for outreach, mentoring and donation drives.

    47 active participants

    AALEKH Club

    Promotes creativity through traditional and digital art, emphasizes sustainable art from recycled materials, and runs art workshops at NGOs under the mentorship of Ms Simi.

    32 regulars

    KALAPI Club

    A space to express emotions through dance and music. Members taught music at Thayi Mane, performed at WizQuest 1.0 and led the cultural segment of the 2024 Christmas Carnival.

    28 performers

    Results Achieved

    Tangible and intangible impact across the school community.

    AI in Summer Projects

    Students made creative chapter trailers using AI tools for scripting, editing, voiceovers and effects.

    63.5%

    found trailer-making very engaging

     

    69.8%

    felt more connected to the content of their chapter

     

    42.9%

    gave the top rating (5 of 5) for how much it helped them understand their English chapter

    Intangible results

    Club and NGO collaborations built teamwork, empathy and social responsibility. Students improved consistency, reflection and self-discipline, grew more open to using AI in everyday learning, and built self-belief by stepping into real-world roles with AIRA. The focus shifted from grades to comprehension and skill development.
